Trains

Before the switch hour the trains card shows Home to Work; from then on, Work to Home. Saved only on this phone.

Tap 🔔 beside a station to get a notification 10 minutes before the train you're on reaches it. Mark your train with the circle on the Trains card. Alerts are sent by your home helper and need the dashboard opened from your Home Screen.

Notifications

Notifications are sent by your home helper, so they arrive even when the dashboard is closed. They need the dashboard opened from your Home Screen. The test arrives 5 seconds after you tap, so you can lock your phone first.

Ring cameras

The helper runs on your home PC and fetches the snapshots from Ring. It also controls the Sonos volume on the Home tab. Saved only on this phone. Leave the address blank to show demo tiles.

App buttons

Each button runs a Shortcut with that name. In the Shortcuts app, make one with a single "Open App" action. You can also paste an app link (e.g. one starting with an app's own scheme). Tap "Add shortcut" for a new row (empty rows aren't saved). Tap × to remove one, and drag the ≡ handle to change the order. When you save, each label is looked up on the App Store to find the app's icon. Saved only on this phone.
